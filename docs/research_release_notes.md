# AERIS v0.1.0 — Research Demonstrator

The first complete release of the defined local research demonstrator includes four application workspaces, ten registered evidence reports, persistent telemetry and incident review, an optional freshness worker, explainable advisory abstention and a virtual maintenance laboratory.

## Research highlights

- Frozen official NASA FD001 endpoint comparison; primary MAE 17.346 cycles versus age-reference 29.160 cycles, with asymmetric-score trade-offs.
- Physical bearing development and replication on XJTU-SY and PRONOSTIA; mixed generalization and negative adaptive-replication results retained.
- Reliability audit accepted zero of 112 assessed physical forecasts.
- Measured vibration-state audit: 372 learning transitions, 54 evaluation endpoints, small and mixed improvement over persistence.
- Synthetic sensitivity audit: 54 scenarios, 500 trials per scenario, hypothetical costs and Monte Carlo uncertainty only.

## Install and verify

Download the versioned source ZIP and SHA256SUMS, verify the checksums, extract and follow docs/quickstart.md. The dependency environment and exact revision accompany the deterministic technical report. The release workflow requires clean extracted-archive regression, browser rendering, authenticated review, concurrent ingestion, periodic monitoring and verified database recovery before publication.

## Scope

No calibrated physical digital twin, operational failure probability, real equipment RUL, field savings, production security or machinery safety certification is claimed. The research release is a reproducible local demonstrator, not a production maintenance product. See research/SCIENTIFIC_CONCLUSIONS.md and docs/release_acceptance.md.
