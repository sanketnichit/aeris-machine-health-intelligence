# Focused literature screening — provisional

Question: Does agreement of two speed indications or two diagnostic decisions establish validity of a fixed bearing diagnosis? This is a retrospective methodological case study, not a new general sensor-fusion theory.

| Source | Established contribution / overlap | Consequence for our claim |
|---|---|---|
| JCGM 100:2008, [Guide to expression of uncertainty in measurement](https://doi.org/10.59161/jcgm100-2008e); [VIM 2.47](https://jcgm.bipm.org/vim/en/2.47.html), [VIM 2.41](https://jcgm.bipm.org/vim/en/2.41.html) | Correlated quantities, compatibility and metrological traceability are established concepts. | Do not claim discovery of shared error or calibration need; deterministic bounds here are not GUM standard uncertainties. |
| [Guaranteed consistency between measurements and parameter systems with correlated additive and multiplicative uncertainties (2022)](https://www.oaepublish.com/articles/ces.2022.13) | Feasible sets for bounded correlated errors are established in system estimation. | Scalar projection is a useful specialization, not established mathematical novelty. |
| [Rolling element bearing diagnostics—A tutorial (2011)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymssp.2010.07.017) | Envelope methods, characteristic frequencies and physical slip. | Speed is only one source of uncertainty; do not present geometry or slip as controlled by this model. |
| [El-Yaniv and Wiener (2010)](https://jmlr.csail.mit.edu/papers/v11/el-yaniv10a.html) | Reject option and risk/coverage trade-off. | Abstention itself is not novel; zero accepted predictions makes retained error undefined. |
| [A Physics-Guided Statistical Framework for Bearing Fault Diagnosis Under Unit Variability and Rotational Speed Uncertainty (2026)](https://doi.org/10.3390/machines14101140) | Order displacement from speed uncertainty, physical screening, calibrated evidence and Paderborn cases. | No first speed-uncertain diagnosis claim; distinguish fixed-spectrum shared-error sensitivity and lack of calibration. |
| [Zhao et al., Support-Bounded Confidence-First Selective Diagnosis (2026)](https://doi.org/10.3390/pr14203325) | Separates applicability from acceptance under speed and source shifts. | No first selective cross-source diagnosis claim; our case study examines deterministic latent shared error rather than confidence ranking. |
| [Physics-Guided Representation Learning and Selective Domain Adaptation (2026)](https://www.mdpi.com/2504-4990/8/11/348) | Cross-condition physics-guided representations and selective adaptation. | No superiority comparison without matched data and protocols. |

Screening uses the title/abstract and relevant accessible sections of publisher pages, not a systematic review. Searches: bearing rotational speed uncertainty selective diagnosis; correlated bounded error set-membership consistency; bearing cross-source selective transfer; JCGM compatibility and traceability. No exact duplicate identified in this limited screening; absence from results is not evidence of novelty. Before submission, expand citation-chaining in metrology, interval analysis, analytical redundancy and bearing diagnostics; obtain human expert review of framing and source attribution. No claim of a first method, state of the art, or confirmed publication gap.
