# Paderborn pre-scoring cohort and archive-document inventory

The publisher paper (2016, Tables 4, 5 and 7, pp. 5, 6 and 8) lists 32 bearing states. The registry transcribes specimen identity, origin and component-level label, not damage severity or exact geometry. Original publisher index has 32 archives; the versioned Zenodo mirror 15845309 lists 31 and lacks KA08. Record that gap rather than silently substituting another archive.

Primary cohort: all six undamaged reference states plus the eleven accelerated-test damaged specimens whose tabulated damaged bearing component is exclusively inner or outer ring. This includes multiple or distributed damages on that component; it is not a single-point-only cohort. Mixed KB23/24/27 are separately unresolved; all artificial damages are a separate future evaluation population. The three structural pilot specimens remain marked, and the other 14 are separately identifiable. No waveform outcomes informed this selection.

Inventory all 17 primary archives against mirror MD5 and record actual SHA-256; enumerate all expected 80 MAT filenames per specimen and non-MAT member names, sizes and eligible document fingerprints. No MAT waveform is loaded in this inventory, no document content is inferred from names, no diagnosis is performed. Delete downloaded raw archives after inspection; publish only metadata. Expected 1,360 filename entries. The individual geometry and timebase remain unresolved until evidence supports them. No calibration or performance claim.
