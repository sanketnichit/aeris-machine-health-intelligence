Freeze before inspecting signal outcomes: baseline_1–3 and InnerRaceFault_vload_1–7, OuterRaceFault_vload_1–7 (17 recordings; first five variable-load records in train_data, last two in test_data; first two baseline in train_data and third in test_data). The source's train/test names are storage locations only: all 17 records are evaluation-only for AERIS. Exclude the three fixed-load outer fault recordings to keep one variable-load series per fault type. No rolling-element-fault controls exist in this subset.

Read bearing.gs and bearing.sr; verify expected rate 97656 Hz for baseline and 48828 Hz for variable-load faults, and shaft rate 25 Hz. Source top-level BPFI, BPFO and BSF give frequencies in Hz, with BSF doubled for the rolling-element candidate to match CWRU's doubled-spin convention. Require positive finite frequencies and verify consistency with published approximate frequencies 118.875, 81.125 and 63.91 Hz (tolerance 0.05 Hz). No label-specific frequency choices. Resample all signals to exactly 12000 Hz with scipy.signal.resample_poly and default Kaiser 5 window, constant padding. First four complete 8192-sample windows (32768 effective samples per file, 557056 total). Four rather than six windows because variable-load recordings last three seconds; this is a protocol difference from CWRU. Preserve the same 2000–5000 Hz fourth-order Butterworth zero-phase bandpass, Hilbert magnitude, periodic Hann, normalization, first three harmonics and ±2 bins. Use median fractions.

Transfer exact registered CWRU policy floor 0.00974446327636002 and relative margin 0.80 without fitting on MFPT. Verify the registered source evidence before use. Keep all three competing orders in forced and selective ranking, including ball. Primary: fault-only coverage, incorrect accepted, retained error (null if zero accepted), and forced errors; normal-only count of incorrect accepted fault suggestions. Also report per-class accepted/withheld outcomes. No classification of abstention as healthy. No threshold tuning, no alternative filter selection, no statistical independence claims. Repeated recordings from one rig may share hardware. MFPT has different sensor acquisition, speed, geometry, sample rates and load conditions; resampling and shortening observation time may affect transfer. No real-world diagnosis, calibration, RUL or unknown-fault validation. Keep negative outcomes and source fingerprints, execution revision and full window results.
