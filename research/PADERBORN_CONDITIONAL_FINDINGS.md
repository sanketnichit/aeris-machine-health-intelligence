The frozen experiment completed, including 13 targeted tests. All 17 specimen fact sheets yielded four unique geometry candidates after the count notation correction (8 rolling elements each). Registered archive, fact sheet and structural pilot fingerprints were verified. No visual geometry verification or actual clock calibration was performed.

Each timing interpretation scored the same 68 recordings (44 damaged, 24 healthy), four conditions per specimen. The results were identical at the decision level for all 68 recordings, including all nine accepted suggestions.

| Timing hypothesis | Correct fault | Wrong fault | Fault abstention | Healthy false suggestion |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Nominal uniform index | 6/44 | 0/44 | 38/44 | 3/24 |
| Stored-time interpolation | 6/44 | 0/44 | 38/44 | 3/24 |

On the 56 nonpilot recordings, each hypothesis yielded 3 correct of 36 damaged recordings, 33 abstentions, and 3 false suggestions on 20 healthy recordings. All healthy false suggestions came from K003 (three conditions). Three of 11 damaged specimens received any correct suggestion: KA04 (3), KA16 (2), KI18 (1). The 900 rpm condition produced no suggestions at all. The remaining 59 decisions per hypothesis were withheld for competing orders. Repeated conditions are not independent specimens, and the two timing hypotheses are paired comparisons, not a doubled independent population.

The frozen rule is not reliably transferable to this physical population: coverage is low and false suggestions occur on a healthy specimen. No threshold changes, new model fitting or claims of diagnostic safety are justified. Decision agreement does not establish equivalence of waveforms or physical timebase validity. All outcomes remain conditional on nominal shaft speed and extracted geometry, and operational authorization is explicitly false.

The bounded external research gate is closed with a negative conditional outcome. Calibrated external diagnostic validation remains unresolved and belongs to a separate prospective program. No raw third-party files are redistributed.
