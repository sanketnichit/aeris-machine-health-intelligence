# MFPT continuous order uncertainty — frozen v1

Development response to the observed MFPT speed-metadata failures. This is a retrospective audit on the same 17 MFPT recordings; not independent confirmation of the revised method.

Verify the source hashes from the registered MFPT transfer report, use the original four 8192-sample effective windows per record, the same 12 kHz rate, fixed bandpass, Hilbert envelope and spectral normalization. Preserve frozen CWRU floor and margin. No threshold fit or new fault classes.

Let declared harmonic centers equal reference centers times (1+e), with e in {-0.05,-0.01,0,0.01,0.05}. The point comparator uses the declared centers. Two independently specified example uncertainty magnitudes u in {0.01,0.05} define a true-center multiplier interval [1/(1+u),1/(1-u)] relative to the declared centers. This interval contains the reference centers if abs(e)<=u. These are experimental bounds, not certified speed tolerances. Do not infer actual speed from labels or spectral peaks.

For each order and harmonic, compute guaranteed bins inside the +/-2 FFT-bin band throughout the interval, and possible bins inside that band at any scale in the interval. Union guaranteed sets and possible sets separately, then sum normalized spectral powers. Guaranteed-set energy is a conservative lower bound; possible-set energy is an upper bound. Take medians of window bounds separately. Accept a unique order only when its lower median exceeds the score floor and every competing upper median, and the relative margin against the largest competing upper median exceeds or equals the frozen margin (conservative numerical slack). Otherwise withhold. This is a sufficient condition for stability over a common scalar interval in the idealized fixed-spectrum model, not a necessary condition, formal floating-point proof, or calibrated probability.

Report point and bounded-rule outcomes across five speed errors and both uncertainty magnitudes, including correct accepted, incorrect accepted, withheld, normal false suggestions, lost correct and prevented incorrect suggestions. Separately flag whether the declared interval includes the reference speed. No claim about unmodeled variation, time-varying shaft speed, clock-dependent filtering or aliasing. Use all 17 records, 10 scenario combinations, 170 record-scenario comparisons; same source windows reused. The revised rule may withhold all cases at wide intervals; preserve this result. No retuning or promotion as field policy. Raw waveforms not redistributed; MFPT attribution and restrictions apply.
