# XJTU reliability audit — exploratory protocol

Use fixed observed ages 20, 50, 100, 200, 500, 1000, excluding the final recorded observation. Hold out one bearing or one operating condition. For held-out bearings, deterministically reserve five remaining bearings for calibration (seed 100+fold), fit on the other nine. For held-out conditions, reserve one remaining condition for calibration (sorted condition index fold mod 2) and fit on the other. No evaluation bearing is used for fitting or calibration.

Candidate: fixed 2x–4x vibration ramp, as defined in XJTU_SIGNAL_GATE_PROTOCOL.md, selected for this exploratory audit without awaiting the gate experiment. Fit HGB as in the previous audit. Calibrate absolute residuals separately at each landmark using surviving calibration bearings. Nominal 80% intervals require at least four independent calibration observations; nominal 90% requires nine and is unavailable by design. Return null if insufficient support. Marginal coverage assumes exchangeability, which is not assured for bearing populations and especially not for held-out conditions. Selection on interval width/disagreement does not preserve conformal coverage guarantees.

Review triggers: history below five observations; missing finite 80% radius; interval width exceeding the fitting-bearing median lifetime; absolute statistical/learned prediction difference exceeding half that median lifetime. These are illustrative heuristics, not calibrated risk thresholds. Retain all candidate predictions for audit, but suppress advisory output when any trigger fires. Report retained fraction, accepted and rejected MAE, unconditional candidate and age-reference MAE, interval availability and empirical coverage by landmark; reason counts can overlap. Do not pool landmarks or claim that abstention improves risk unless observed. No policy selection, safety guarantee or independent confirmation. The XJTU development data have already been observed.
