# CWRU selective diagnosis — frozen v1

Question: does a development-selected rejection rule reduce erroneous fault-order suggestions on a previously unexamined CWRU fault severity? This is same-platform severity transfer, not independent-platform or industrial validation.

Development: registered cwru_extension_summary.json, 16 seeded-fault and four normal recordings. No earlier two-order audit used in fitting. Evaluation: official 12 kHz drive-end recordings with 0.021-inch defects, centered outer race, four loads per type: inner 209–212, ball 222–225, outer 234–237. These file IDs do not overlap either previous audit. Official table https://engineering.case.edu/bearingdatacenter/12k-drive-end-bearing-fault-data and geometry https://engineering.case.edu/bearingdatacenter/bearing-information. No evaluation normal controls: all four previously used normal files remain development-only.

Use the exact frozen cwru_extension.evaluate processing (six complete 8192-sample windows per recording, nominal speeds, three defect orders, fixed bandpass and Hilbert transform, per-order medians). Do not choose alternate frequency orders or filter settings after observing new recordings. Inputs to decision rule: three envelope fractions only. The absolute score floor is the maximum of the four development-normal maximum scores (strictly greater required). For a unique top order, margin = (top - runner-up)/top. Ties, zero scores and scores at or below the floor abstain. The candidate margin thresholds are fixed 0, .05, .10, ..., 1.00; acceptance requires margin >= threshold. On development fault records choose the candidate with maximum accepted count among candidates with zero accepted errors and at least four accepted records. Tie-break toward larger threshold. If none qualifies, disable all selective suggestions. This is empirical development selection, not calibrated confidence or a statistical risk guarantee.

Primary evaluation: report accepted, incorrect accepted, abstentions, coverage and retained error (null when none accepted), compared with forced top-order ranking on the same 12 records. Report per-fault outcomes and reasons; forced ties count as errors. No evaluation-label input to the decision. No threshold change or post-hoc primary selection. Evaluation uses 72 windows and 589824 measured samples if all files pass checks. Preserve source hashes and implementation revision, all errors and abstentions.

Severity changes may change signal strength and order visibility; the selected population uses one experimental rig and correlated loads. No diagnosis of normal state, unknown defects, prospective field performance, probability calibration, operational alarm thresholds or remaining-life prediction. Treat accepted outputs as research suggestions only.
