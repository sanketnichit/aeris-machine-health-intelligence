# Paderborn source-intake pilot (pre-diagnostic)

Sources: [original publisher dataset](https://mb.uni-paderborn.de/en/kat/research/bearing-datacenter/data-sets-and-download), [original archive index](https://groups.uni-paderborn.de/kat/BearingDataCenter/), [operating conditions](https://mb.uni-paderborn.de/en/kat/research/bearing-datacenter/operating-conditions), [versioned mirror](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15845309). Original license CC BY-NC 4.0; attribution Christian Lessmeier et al., KAt-DataCenter, Chair of Design and Drive Technology, Paderborn University; Lessmeier et al., Condition Monitoring of Bearing Damage in Electromechanical Drive Systems Using Motor Current Signals, PHM Europe 2016. The mirror is by Jurgen van den Hoogen (2025), not the original publisher.

Freeze the pilot to K001, KA04 and KI04 before waveform inspection. These three archives are only a parser and acquisition pilot, not a representative evaluation population. Verify against the mirror's published MD5 (legacy byte identity, not a cryptographic authenticity guarantee), record SHA-256, bounded download, exact 80 measurement filenames per specimen (four settings times 20 repetitions). Use original publisher download first and separately identified pinned mirror fallback. Extract only repetition 1 for each setting into memory; verify MAT variable name and the explicitly named vibration_1 channel. Record the observed number of finite nonconstant real samples (bounded to 2 through 1,000,000); flag mismatch with the nominal 256,000 samples (four seconds at 64 kHz), report raw unit and raster metadata without inferring their interpretation. No padding, truncation or resampling. No physical clock calibration is established by sample count. Do not substitute motor current or a positional channel.

This is a structural source audit of 240 inventory names and 12 selected recordings. No RMS, spectral scores, fault decisions, label-based preprocessing or performance metrics. No raw archives or waveform data in the repository or CI artifact. Fact sheets, geometry, clock and acquisition-chain verification remain gates before diagnostic scoring. Failure to acquire or parse blocks the pilot; no silent skipping.
