# Changelog

## 0.1.0-rc.1 — research release candidate

### Research
- AI4I generator-rule comparison, sensor corruption and operating-regime stress audits.
- NASA FD001 unit-disjoint prognostics, landmark calibration, paired comparisons, asymmetric risk and hypothetical maintenance replay.
- Frozen official FD001 evaluation with 100 simulated test engines.
- XJTU-SY physical vibration evaluation on 15 bearings and three conditions.
- Causal vibration-gating comparison and selective reliability audit.
- Fixed PRONOSTIA replication with six training and eleven evaluation bearings.
- Measured physical replay from three previously evaluated PRONOSTIA bearings.

### Application and engineering
- Read-only research command center with nine on-demand workspaces.
- Separate AI4I prediction workspace; deferred model and explanation dependencies.
- Native dark industrial theme.
- Evidence fingerprint verification, fail-closed display and deterministic Markdown technical report.
- Regression tests, Chromium browser smoke workflow and screenshot artifacts.
- Installation, architecture and demonstration documentation.

### Known limitations
- AI4I is synthetic snapshot classification, not prospective field failure prediction.
- Physical adaptive superiority did not replicate on PRONOSTIA.
- The evaluated XJTU review policy accepted no forecasts; no finite 90% intervals were available.
- Official and independent evaluation outcomes are already observed and must not be used for new independent claims.
- No production safety, live equipment connection, measured cost savings or state-of-the-art status established.
- This is a candidate version, not a published release tag. Release packaging and fresh-environment validation are separate gates.
